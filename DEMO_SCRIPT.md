# 90-second demo script

**0–12s — Problem.** “Every payment has potential, but comparing cards based on flashy points multipliers hides the real economics.”

**12–26s — Dashboard.** “Meet VALORA. Rather than showing affiliate-sponsored winners, it calculates estimated net annual value based on your real-world spending assumptions.”

**26–42s — Financial Twin.** “I'll choose a frequent traveler profile and edit monthly spending, FX exposure, and lounge visits. VALORA recalculates instantly. Notice that we use fictional cards, not unsourced real-world offers.”

**42–58s — Compare & Simulator.** “The Compare page separates gross rewards, annual fees, FX costs, interest, benefits, and first-year bonuses. Now I change travel spending in the simulator, and the rankings update live.”

**58–72s — Wallet.** “For an everyday grocery purchase, the wallet optimizer looks only at cards I already own, respects annual category caps, and suggests the strongest estimated reward.”

**72–84s — Evidence Ledger.** “Every recommendation has an evidence ledger: inputs, formulas, source provenance, and missing information. The calculation engine, not the AI, controls the math.”

**84–90s — Close.** “VALORA: every payment has potential. Make yours count.”
