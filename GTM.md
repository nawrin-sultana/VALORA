# Product positioning & go-to-market strategy

**Positioning:** Explainable financial decision intelligence that optimizes realistic customer value instead of rewards hype or affiliate conversion. The defensible wedge is a deterministic rewards economics model with an auditable evidence ledger, counterfactual simulation and a wallet-first retention recommendation.

**Ideal initial segment:** Canadian professionals with two or more cards and diverse everyday spending; follow-on segments include frequent travelers and small business owners. Business profiles must never be compared with consumer products without explicit eligibility checks.

**Acquisition experiments:** Publish educational calculators with shareable scenario summaries; form content partnerships with trusted financial education publishers; build search landing pages for “is the annual fee worth it?” and “cashback vs points”; host unbiased demo workshops. No issuer affiliate integration should influence ranking.

**North-star metric:** Verified incremental annual customer value, adjusted for confidence and realized redemption. Supporting metrics: completed profiles, repeat wallet decisions, percentage of cards kept instead of newly acquired, evidence-ledger opens, and user-confirmed benefit realization.

**Distribution roadmap:** Demo → verified Canadian catalog → opt-in issuer data sync → customer value reporting → qualified publisher integrations. Compliance, card-source governance, consent, privacy and disclosures precede monetization.

**Monetization principles:** Transparent subscriptions or B2B licensing preferred over undisclosed referrals. Any future affiliate relationships must be prominently disclosed and segregated from rankings.
